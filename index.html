Site Visit Checklist

🔴 Priority — Confirm First (ask on arrival)

Get permissions: what we can/can’t film, measure, or open.
Files today? Latest CAD (plan/elevation/sections with dimensions).
Manufacturer 3D? STEP/IGES/SolidWorks/Max/Maya/Blender/FBX.
Device 3-views? Front/side/top drawings or tech sheets with sizes.
BOM (names, models, quantities, locations) & any manuals with dimensions.
Signage assets (AI/PNG/SVG for labels/warnings/UI panels).

Capture the space

One continuous walkthrough video with quick voice notes (area names, floor/level).
Room stills: from each corner and toward each corner.
Include ceiling, columns, ducts, rails, cable trays, exits, safety points.

Capture each key device

Three views: front, side, top/oblique.
Open/closed: doors, lids, guards, rails.
Close-ups: labels, warnings, UI panels (flat, sharp, no glare).
Flat texture shots for big planes (walls/floor/guards); include A4 sheet or ruler in one shot for scale.

Measure (only if CAD is missing/doubtful)

Room L×W, clear height (beam/ceiling), corridor width, door clear W/H.
Device footprint + height and working envelope (swing/slide).
Relative distances: device↔wall/column; rail/pipe heights.

Before you leave

3-minute recap with client: what we got, what’s missing, who sends what, when.
Check photo/video focus & coverage.
Back up twice (drive + cloud). Use clear names: TYT_CII_Item_001.
